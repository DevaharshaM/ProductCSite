The reason this exists

Why Pravaha Path?

Learning engineering is often fragmented.
There are tutorials, courses, and documentation — but very little guidance on how concepts connect together over time.

Pravaha Path is an attempt to explore a more structured approach to engineering growth. Not as a platform. But as a system that evolves with the learner.

The name comes from the idea of flow — a current that moves with intention, not just speed. Engineering growth, when given the right structure, can feel like that.
